Dans les cendres du temps : application de la méthode de répartition spatiale à une couche incendiée à Satrianum (Basilicate, Italie) In the Ashes of Time: Applying the Spatial Distribution Method to a Burnt Layer at Satrianum (Basilicata, Italy)

Author: Acius Exio
Institution: Université Rennes 2, France
Year: 2025
Master's degree: Mention Bien · Dissertation: 18/20
Site: Torre di Satriano (Satrianum), Tito, Basilicata, Italy

Abstract

This dissertation tests the spatial distribution method, widely used in prehistoric archaeology, on a medieval fortified site: sector CF 104 at Satrianum, a burnt abandonment deposit excavated over three field seasons (2022–2024). Based on a corpus of 1,062 total-station records corresponding to 2,272 individual finds, three-dimensional recording and spatial density analysis are used to reconstruct the room's internal spatial organisation and the processes — collapse, displacement, deposition — that produced its final archaeological distribution.

The analysis suggests internal spatial differentiation within CF 104. Patterns in find density and elevation are considered alongside possible circulation areas, perishable partitions and structural supports, informing an interpretation of the room as a service space. These architectural and functional readings remain hypotheses. Beyond these results, the dissertation reflects critically on the method itself: its dependence on data quality, its limits in disturbed or low-density contexts, and its value as an interpretive tool rather than a source of certainty.
